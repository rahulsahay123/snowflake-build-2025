=======================================================================
        Live Demo Screenshots – Snowflake Insurance Claims Agent
=======================================================================

These screenshots were taken LIVE on December 2025 from the actual
agent running on real structured data (FACT_CLAIMS, DIM_POLICY, etc.)
and real unstructured .txt reports (police_report_*.txt, medical_report_*.txt,
property_assessment_*.txt, adjuster_notes_*.txt).

No mock data. No hallucinations. 100% real.

1. demo-screenshot-1.png
   → Claims Adjuster: “For claim CLM-2025-001590 show claim amount,
     settlement amount, processing days and quote the exact diagnosis
     from the medical report.”

2. demo-screenshot-2.png
   → Claims Manager: “Show settlement ratio and average processing days
     for all property claims in the last 12 months, then quote the
     surveyor’s comment on hail vs wear-and-tear from CLM-2024-002286.”

3. demo-screenshot-3.png
   → Policyholder: “Hi, this is customer for claim CLM-2025-000898.
     How much has been approved and read me what the police officer
     wrote about who was at fault.”

4. demo-screenshot-4.png
   → SIU Investigator: “Find customers with 3 or more claims in the
     last 2 years and for the top one, quote any mention of
     ‘pre-existing condition’ from their medical reports.”

5. demo-screenshot-5.png
   → Actuary: “Calculate ultimate loss ratio for 2025 (settlement +
     80% of unsettled ÷ premium) and quote the adjuster’s liability
     opinion from the top 3 largest open claims.”

All prompts use real claim IDs that exist in the FACT_CLAIMS table
and quote text directly from the uploaded .txt files.
